# VISTAAR — SEO Keyword Map & Search Intent Architecture

**Target Geography**: India (Pan-India, with deep focus on Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 trade hubs)  
**Target Audiences**: Indian MSME owners, retail shopkeepers, wholesale merchants, FMCG distributors, hardware/electronics dealers.  
**Research Grounding**: Intent analysis, SERP qualitative landscape, Google Search Console query patterns, and competitor taxonomy.

> **Note on Research Confidence**: Search volumes and ranking difficulties are qualitative assessments based on SERP observation and Indian SaaS industry standards. In accordance with safety rules, no third-party synthetic volume metrics are fabricated.
