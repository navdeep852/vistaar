## 4. E-E-A-T & Editorial Quality Guidelines

To comply with Google's Quality Rater Guidelines (E-E-A-T):
1. **Experience**: Ground all workflows in actual retail realities (power fluctuations, rushed evening footfall, split UPI/Cash payments).
2. **Expertise**: Author content with practical accounting and retail trade understanding. Avoid vague high-level fluff.
3. **Authoritativeness**: Provide clear, verifiable facts regarding Indian tax slabs (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and HSN/SAC directory procedures.
4. **Trustworthiness**: Clearly distinguish software capabilities from statutory tax advice. Include transparent publishing and update dates. No fabricated claims, ratings, or artificial user numbers.
